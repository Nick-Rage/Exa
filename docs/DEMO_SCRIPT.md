# Demo script — sell the Monday, not the features

## Setup

```bash
npm run dev   # http://localhost:3000
```

Hard refresh. Queue empty. Ready to click **Show Maya's Monday**.

---

## 0–5 min · Slides

`docs/SLIDES.md` — land: she spends 3–4 hours being the research engine; Google only gives pages.

---

## 5–8 min · Empty state (don’t skip this)

Point at the Without / With columns on screen.

> “This is not a search demo. It’s Maya’s Monday. Without Exa she is the one opening every PDF. With Exa she reviews a shortlist.”

One line from the header:

> “Google gives her pages to read. Exa gives her a shortlist already checked against her rules.”

---

## 8–14 min · Click “Show Maya's Monday”

Narrate while it runs:

1. **Left column fills** with keyword results — “This is regular search. Every row still says she has to open and judge it.”
2. **Theater streams domains** — “This is Exa actually working — those are live pages it’s opening. Nothing here is a fake spinner.”
3. **Right column lands** with Qualified / Out — “Same intent. Different job left for her.”

Read the summary: sources read → records → qualified · seconds · $0.025.

---

## 14–22 min · Open one Qualified + one Out

**Qualified (e.g. KCTCS):**  
Walk the five gates. Read one evidence sentence aloud. Show grounding citation.  

> “She didn’t find this by skimming forty PDFs. Exa did the skimming. She decides Pursue.”

**Out (airport / hardware / not primeable):**  

> “Keyword search loves this title. Exa fails Primeable or hardware and explains why. That’s hours she doesn’t spend.”

Mark Pursue on the good one → **Export for Thursday**.

---

## 22–28 min · Day-to-day loop

- **What’s new next Monday** — exclusions, not the same hunt again  
- **Judge my feed** (optional) — she already pays for GovWin; Exa qualifies rows  

---

## Objections

**“Why not Google?”**  
Point at the split. Left = pages. Right = judged records. That’s the answer.

**“Why not HigherGov?”**  
Coverage is their game. Exa’s game is judgement + obscure sources + evidence into a workflow. Mode “Judge my feed” is the honest land.

**“Can she trust it?”**  
Verdict is computed from gates; every gate shows evidence; citations from Exa grounding. She still hits Pursue — Exa doesn’t bid.

---

## Closing line

> Exa doesn’t need to beat Google at browsing. It needs to win the Mondays where the output has to be a decision-ready shortlist — not another page of links.
