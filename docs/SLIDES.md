# Three slides — then the split screen

---

## Slide 1 — Her day, not our product

**Maya Chen · Capture Manager · Meridian Identity**

Thursday she must defend a bid/no-bid shortlist.

Monday today: 3–4 hours of Google, SAM.gov, PDFs, and a spreadsheet.  
She is doing research work that software should do.

> Speak: the problem isn’t “finding the internet.” It’s that **judgement doesn’t scale**, and she’s the one scaling it by hand.

---

## Slide 2 — Why Google can’t do this job

Her questions are not keyword questions:

- Is this still open?  
- Is identity the *subject*, or one bullet in a dispatch RFP?  
- Is “access control” SSO — or door hardware?

Google returns **pages to read**.  
She needs **records already checked**, with evidence, ready for Thursday.

> Line: Google helps a person browse. Exa helps a workflow decide what deserves a human.

---

## Slide 3 — What Exa changes on Monday

Same intent she would type into search.

**Exa Agent** researches, opens notices, judges her five bid rules, returns a shortlist with citations.

Her job shrinks to: Pursue / Watch / Pass — ~15 minutes.

Cost of a run: about **$0.025**.

> Then go live: “I’m going to show her Monday with regular search and with Exa — same question, two outcomes.”
